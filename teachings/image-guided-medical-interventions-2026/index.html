465-0970-00L Image Guided Medical Interventions (Autumn 2026)

Computer-assisted methods and technologies in interventional medicine: image-guided radiotherapy, from using medical images to model patient anatomy to intraoperative navigation and registration. Covers imaging/image-processing fundamentals, localization and navigation technologies, and on-line treatment verification. Bastien Golomer is the teaching assistant for this course (not the instructor).

Instructor: Giovanni Fattori

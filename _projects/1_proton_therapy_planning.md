**MSc thesis, EPFL / AMAS Lab, Paul Scherrer Institut (2023).**

## Abstract

This study delves into two distinct strategies for addressing the challenge of predicting beam angles of non-coplanar fields in proton therapy, applied to cranial tumours. The initial approach utilises Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), investigated through both supervised and unsupervised learning paradigms. While the former leverages annotated data, the latter relies on a heuristic loss function based on dosimetric prescriptions. These two methods are assessed for their ability to forecast angles for a single field only, but their performances remain unsatisfactory, primarily due to limitations in available patient data. Conversely, the second approach introduces a novel gradient descent variation tailored to angles prediction for three non-coplanar fields. By harnessing the previously mentioned heuristic loss, this approach yields promising outcomes within reasonable computational time, spanning both single and multiple-field dose distributions optimisations. The predicted plans' efficacy is benchmarked against clinical reference plans, albeit without accounting for robustness with respect to patient positioning in this initial implementation.

Supervised by Prof. Dr. Andreas Pautz (EPFL/ETHZ/PSI) and Dr. Andreas Adelmann (PSI/ETHZ), in the Accelerator Modelling and Advanced Simulations (AMAS) laboratory at PSI.

**Bachelor Thesis — Chandra Tresch (July 2025).**

## Abstract

Background and Motivation: This project aimed to develop a pipeline for extraction and subsequent analysis of radiomics features derived from apparent diffusion coefficient (ADC) maps. Exploratory testing of the pipeline was performed on a cohort of brain tumor patients, investigating the correlation of local treatment failure (LF) with radiomics features derived from pre-treatment ADC distributions within the gross tumor volume (GTV).

Materials and Methods: A framework for rigid and affine multimodal image registration of ADC maps to computed tomography (CT) images was implemented alongside methods for harmonization of ADC maps. The patient cohort comprised 11 chordoma and chondrosarcoma patients treated with intensity-modulated proton therapy, with 3 patients exhibiting LF. For each patient, 53 radiomics features were extracted from the ADC map of the GTV using pre-implemented methods. Spearman rank correlation coefficients were computed to assess the association between each feature and LF, with statistical significance evaluated via permutation testing.

Results: Exploratory analysis showed that overall raw ADC values were significantly elevated within the tumor volume. A broader trend of increased heterogeneity in the tumor was observed to correlate positively with LF; however, no individual radiomics feature reached statistical significance.

Conclusion: The developed pipeline offers a reproducible method for extracting ADC-derived radiomics features. Although no statistically significant associations with LF were found in this exploratory cohort, the observed trends warrant further investigation of the prognostic potential of ADC-based radiomics in brain tumor outcomes.

Supervised at the Center for Proton Therapy, Paul Scherrer Institut.

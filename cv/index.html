Projects

  • 2023 - 2023
    Beam Angle Selection of Non-Coplanar Fields in Proton Therapy

    MSc thesis, EPFL / AMAS Lab, PSI (2023). This study delves into two distinct strategies for addressing the challenge of predicting beam angles of non-coplanar fields in proton therapy, applied to cranial tumours. The initial approach utilises Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), investigated through both supervised and unsupervised learning paradigms; these were assessed for their ability to forecast angles for a single field only, but performance remained unsatisfactory, primarily due to limitations in available patient data. The second approach introduces a novel gradient descent variation tailored to angles prediction for three non-coplanar fields, yielding promising outcomes within reasonable computational time. The predicted plans’ efficacy was benchmarked against clinical reference plans.

  • 2022 - 2023
    Differentiable Influence Matrix Calculation Using Automatic Differentiation

    Internship project, AMAS Lab / Center for Proton Therapy, PSI (2022–2023). Rewrote FIonA’s dose-influence-matrix calculation (water-equivalent depth and Dij elements) as a differentiable pipeline in Julia using automatic differentiation (Enzyme.jl), to enable gradient-based optimization of spot positions, energies, and field angles.

    Road Segmentation with a Double U-Net

    Machine Learning course, EPFL (2021). Studied a new convolutional neural network architecture for road segmentation of satellite images.

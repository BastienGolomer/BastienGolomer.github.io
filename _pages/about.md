Welcome! I'm a PhD student working on outcome models of adverse effects occurring after [proton therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_therapy). 

 My current work focuses on prediction models for radiation-induced contrast enhancements in the temporal lobes after skull-base tumour treatment (a normal tissue toxicity), as well as prediction models for local failure of the same tumour type.

Through conference experiences I had the chance to join the [Early Career Researcher subcommittee of PTCOG](https://ptcog.online/subcommittee/ecr-subcommitte/) (Particle Therapy Co-Operative Group) and connect more with the community.

This site contains research updates, such as paper publications, conference talks, students projects, teaching information and sometimes occasional post on things I'm experimenting with.


My academic path started at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) where I graduated with a major in Physics and a minor in Data Science, and specialized for a time in particle accelerator physics at EPFL's [LPAP](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lpap/). That led me into medical physics, and I joined the [Paul Scherrer Institute](https://www.psi.ch/en) for an internship at the [Center for Proton Therapy (CPT)](https://www.psi.ch/en/protontherapy) and the [AMAS group](http://amas.web.psi.ch/), which got extended into a Master's Thesis. But I wanted to further explore the field of Proton Therapy and started my PhD project at CPT.
